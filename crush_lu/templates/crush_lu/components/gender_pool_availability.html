{% comment %} Per-gender seat availability for an event with active gender caps. Gender-balanced events (speed dating and friends) enforce capacity per pool, not against the total: `max_participants_m` / `_f` / `_nb`. Showing only the total tells a member seats are free that are not free for *them* -- they register against "4 spots left", event_register waitlists them because their own pool is full, and nothing on the page ever explained why (#866). Args: pools MeetupEvent.get_gender_pool_availability(): a list of dicts with key / label / limit / confirmed / remaining / is_full. Empty for an event without caps, so callers branch on it directly and keep their total-only display. user_pool Optional. The viewing member's own row out of `pools`, or None -- anonymous visitors and members with no profile gender get the per-pool chips but no personal line, because we cannot say which pool would take them. viewer_registration Optional. The viewer's own non-cancelled EventRegistration. When they already hold a seat their own registration is part of what filled the pool, so "join the waitlist" would be advice on a page that is simultaneously showing them their ticket -- and if they are already waitlisted, they are on it. Either way the personal line is suppressed; the chips stay, since the counts are still true. waitlist_reason Optional. `"total"`, `"pool"` or None, straight from views_events._registration_outlook(). It picks *which* explanation the personal line gives, because `user_pool.is_full` cannot: that flag folds the pool's own cap together with a total or reserved-premium block, so a viewer stopped by overall capacity was told their gender group was full when it was not. Reading the same value the registration page reads keeps all three surfaces -- chip, CTA and the flash after POST -- naming the same cause, and "total" wins a tie there for the same reason it does here: when the whole event is full, that is the plainer thing to tell someone. {% endcomment %} {% load i18n %}
