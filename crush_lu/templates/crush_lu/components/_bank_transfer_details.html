The QR is an EPC "scan to pay" code with no amount: the member picks one in their banking app. Not every banking app scans it, and a member on their phone cannot scan their own screen, so every field also has a copy button. The SVG is drawn here from module coordinates rather than marked safe in Python. It stays black on white in dark mode, because scanners need the contrast and the quiet zone. On a phone it comes after the details: nobody scans their own screen, so there the copy buttons are the way in. items-start, not items-center + sm:items-start: base.html's critical inline CSS defines an unlayered .items-center, which beats any sm: override. {% endcomment %}
