{% load i18n %} {% comment %} What a curated speed-dating event's age range means. The event's min/max age only says who may apply: each date is with someone whose age is inside the member's own range and who has the member's age inside theirs (crush_lu.matching.passes_event_hard_filters). A bare "Ages 20-70" read as the spread of your dates and put people off applying. Expects `age_range` from views_events._curated_age_range: the event's range plus, for a logged-in viewer, only the viewer's OWN range. Never another member's. Without an own range the bar shows a labelled example band. {% endcomment %}
