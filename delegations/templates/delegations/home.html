{% trans "Frequently Asked Questions" %}

{% trans "Employment Rights & Staff Delegations in Luxembourg" %}

{% trans "What should I do first if I receive a dismissal letter in Luxembourg?" %}
{% trans "If you are dismissed with notice (licenciement avec préavis) in Luxembourg, you have strictly 1 month from notification to request the precise reasons by registered letter (lettre recommandée). Once the employer replies (or for immediate dismissal), a 3-month deadline applies to contest unfair dismissal." %}
{% trans "When is a Staff Delegation (Délégation du personnel) mandatory in Luxembourg?" %}
{% trans "Under Article L.411-1 of the Luxembourg Labour Code, every private-sector company employing at least 15 workers in Luxembourg is legally required to have an elected Staff Delegation to represent and defend employees." %}
{% trans "Am I protected against dismissal during sick leave in Luxembourg?" %}
{% trans "Yes, under Article L.121-6 of the Labour Code, you are protected against dismissal for up to 26 consecutive weeks, provided you inform your employer on the 1st day of absence and submit your medical certificate no later than the 3rd day." %}
{% trans "How can I report workplace harassment (mobbing) in Luxembourg?" %}
{% trans "Since the Luxembourg Law of 29 March 2023 on moral harassment, employers must stop harassment immediately and protect victims and witnesses from retaliation. You can seek assistance from your Staff Delegation and escalate to the Inspection du travail et des mines (ITM)." %}
{% trans "Can my employer change my salary, working hours, or role without my consent?" %}
{% trans "No. Under Article L.121-7 of the Luxembourg Labour Code, any substantial modification of an essential clause to the employee's detriment must follow the same strict procedure and notice period as a dismissal." %}
{% trans "Is my request on Delegations.lu confidential from my employer?" %}
{% trans "Yes. Submitting an employment issue assessment on Delegations.lu is strictly confidential. Your employer is never notified, and we recommend using a personal email address for complete peace of mind." %}
