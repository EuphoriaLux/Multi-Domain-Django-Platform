Meet people in Luxembourg, in person and online. Crush.lu brings local events and member connections together in one app.

Explore upcoming events, create your profile, and stay in touch with the people you meet. Crush Connect offers a more thoughtful way to discover members and build a connection, with access based on your profile and verification status.

With Crush.lu you can:
• Discover and book local events
• Create and manage your profile
• Complete the available verification steps
• Explore eligible Crush Connect features and manage your connections
• Receive updates about events and your account
• Adjust privacy settings and request account deletion

Some connection features require verification, event participation, or an invitation. Availability can vary by account. Crush.lu is for adults aged 18 and over.

Need help? Visit https://crush.lu/en/support/
